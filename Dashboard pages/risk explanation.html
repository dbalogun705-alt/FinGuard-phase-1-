Risk Explanation

FinGuard identifies financial pressure by looking at your income, debt and available cash buffer.

Your debt level, monthly expenses, income and cashflow can all contribute to your overall financial risk.

Higher debt and expenses can reduce the amount of money available for savings and unexpected expenses.

You can improve your cash buffer by managing expenses, reducing unnecessary spending and setting aside money regularly.
